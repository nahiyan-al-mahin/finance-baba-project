Finance Baba

Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Finance Baba ("the app") is a personal finance diary built by Mahin Uddin. This policy explains what the app stores and why. It applies to the website and the Android app, which is the same service.

What we store

  • Account: your username, display name, email address, time zone, language and password. The password is stored only as a salted hash and cannot be read.
  • Your finance data: the entries, loans, savings, targets and settings you add.
  • Google sign-in (optional): if you link Google, your Google account email and an identifier.
  • Technical data: sign-in sessions, failed login counts, and anonymous page-view statistics (Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies).

How it is protected

Your entries, loans, savings, targets and profile are encrypted with a key that only your password (or recovery code) can unlock. Administrators cannot read your name or your money. They only see counts, such as how many users exist. All traffic uses HTTPS.

If you turn on monthly email reports, a copy of your key is kept on the server so that it can send your report. You can switch this off at any time in Settings, and the copy is then deleted.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to show your totals and reports, send verification codes, over-target alerts and the monthly reports you asked for, and keep accounts secure. There are no ads. We do not sell or share your data.

Service providers

Hosting (Vercel), the database (Neon), and email delivery (Google Gmail SMTP) process data on our behalf. Exchange rates are fetched from public rate services; no personal data is sent to them.

Your choices

  • Download your reports as PDF at any time.
  • Turn monthly emails on or off in Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Danger zone. This permanently removes your account and all your data.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13.

Contact

Questions? Use the contact email listed on the app's Play Store page.
